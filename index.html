SHINOBI BOUT RULES

Goal: Empty your hand first to claim victory!

Basic Play: Match the current suit element or number on top of the discard pile.

Sequential Runs: You can play multiple cards in one turn if they are the exact same suit and form consecutive numbers (e.g., Fire 3, Fire 4, Fire 5).

Jutsu Cards:

  • Shadow Clone (+2): Opponent draws 2 cards.
  • Reaper Seal (+4 & Skip): Opponent draws 4 cards and loses turn.
